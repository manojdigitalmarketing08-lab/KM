Local SEO questions

Common questions

01Is Local SEO different from regular SEO?

It shares the same base, but local results lean on proximity, your Google profile, reviews and consistent listings. Regular SEO leans on content and links across a wider area.

02We have several branches. Can you handle all of them?

Yes. Each branch gets its own profile, page and tracking so every location can be measured on its own.

03We serve customers at their homes. Does this still work?

It does. Service area businesses can rank for the areas they cover without showing a street address.
