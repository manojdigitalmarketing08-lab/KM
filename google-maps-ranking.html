How Maps decides

Three signals decide the order

Google describes local ranking as relevance, distance and prominence. We can influence two of them directly.

  • RelevanceWe control this

    How well your profile and pages match the search. Categories, services, descriptions and on-page copy all feed this signal.

  • ProminenceWe shape this over time

    How well known and trusted you are. Reviews, links, mentions and a strong website lift it month after month.

  • DistanceFixed by geography

    How close you are to the person searching. We cannot move your shop, but we can build presence in the areas around it.
