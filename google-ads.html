Ads questions

Before you start

01What budget do we need?

It depends on your market's click costs and your target leads per month. We work it out from keyword data and give you a range before you commit.

02Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

We quote a fixed management fee after reviewing the account, so you know the cost each month.

03Who owns the ad account?

You do. Your card and your account stay in your name, and our access can be removed whenever you choose.

04How fast will we see leads?

Often in the first week. The first month is for learning, so expect costs to improve as data builds.
